CSRD · ESRS E1 · SFDR · CAC 40

ESG Lens

Greenhouse gas emissions and climate targets of the 40 CAC 40 companies, read from their FY2025 sustainability statements (CSRD / ESRS E1) and turned into a dataset where every figure can be checked: each value keeps the report page it came from and the row as printed.

The figures an investor needs — Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions and reduction targets — sit in 300 to 1,000-page PDFs with no common layout. ESG Lens reads them directly, measures how often the reading is right, and keeps the evidence so any number can be traced back in one click.
